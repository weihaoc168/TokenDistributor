        Caveats

          Weighted cost

          Every "weighted" figure on this page is computed with one formula, so the tiers compare on a single scale:

          weighted_cost = output + 0.1 × (input + cache_creation) + 0.01 × cache_read

          It is a comparison proxy built from transcript usage records. It is not a bill. The dollars under What it cost are an estimate of one, and they use one formula too:

          cost_usd = (input × input_price + output × output_price + cache_5m × cache_write_price + cache_1h × cache_write_1h_price + cache_read × cache_read_price) ÷ 1,000,000

          Five terms, because cache writes carry two prices and the usage records carry both counts: cache_5m + cache_1h = cache_creation, where the 1-hour writes bill at 2× base input and the 5-minute ones at 1.25×. Charging the whole creation figure at the 5-minute rate would understate every dollar on this page. What the formula still does not model is listed under Caveats.

          Prices used

          USD per 1M tokens, per model, with the source each figure was read from and the date it was read. Five figures per model, because the two cache-write durations are two published prices. Nothing here is inferred from a neighbouring model: a model missing any of the five is billed at nothing and reported as unpriced.

          Graph in force

          The agentic graph exactly as it stood when this page was generated: the model, the fallback under it and the headcount at each tier. It is recorded here because the graph moves — a page read next week must not be reinterpreted against next week's tiers.
